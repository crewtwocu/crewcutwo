Every piece has a story

Chandler’s Emporium is an antiques shop for people who like to linger. We gather furniture, china and porcelain, and the small curiosities in between, and show them the way a gallery would: one room at a time, each with its own plaque.

Take your time. If something catches your eye, ask us about it.
