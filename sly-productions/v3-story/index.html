SUBJECT: Sly Productions

Some stories get buried.
We dig.

Sly Productions makes gritty, documentary-style true-crime stories. We start with a lead, pull the thread, and keep pulling until the whole thing comes up out of the ground.

If you know something, saw something, or have been sitting on a story for years, this is where you put it down on paper.

Procedure

How a file gets opened

  1. 01

    You send the lead

    Pitch the story in your own words. Rough is fine. Details matter more than polish.

  2. 02

    We read every page

    Each submission lands in a closed inbox and gets read. Nothing is published without talking to you first.

  3. 03

    We start digging

    If the story has legs, we get in touch using the details you leave.
